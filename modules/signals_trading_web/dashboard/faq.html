Frequently Asked Questions

Learn how the NEA28V1 Institutional Trade Dashboard works, how trade setups are presented, and how to interpret the information published by the system.

FAQ / INFORMATION

General Questions

WHAT IS NEA28V1?

What is the NEA28V1 Institutional Trade Dashboard?

NEA28V1 is an institutional-style trade setup and market analysis system designed to identify, rank, and present potential trade opportunities. The dashboard provides a centralized view of published trade setups and their current status.

PURPOSE

What is the purpose of the dashboard?

The dashboard is designed to make trade setups easier to review. Instead of requiring users to manually examine every security, the system presents selected setups with information such as direction, entry, stop, target, score, and status.

TIMEFRAME

What is the primary timeframe?

The primary timeframe for the published trade setups is 60 minutes (1H).

Additional timeframes may be used by the underlying analysis system for confirmation and contextual analysis.

TRADE SETUPS

What is a trade setup?

A trade setup represents a security that has met the system's defined conditions for consideration. A setup can include a directional bias, entry price, stop level, target price, score, and confirmation status.

Understanding Trade Setups

DIRECTION

What does Direction mean?

Direction identifies the directional bias of the setup, such as LONG or SHORT.

ENTRY

What does Entry mean?

Entry represents the price level associated with the trade setup. It is the reference price used by the system when defining the potential trade opportunity.

STOP

What does Stop mean?

Stop represents the system's calculated risk-control level for the setup. It is intended to define the price area at which the trade thesis would no longer be considered valid.

TARGET

What does Target mean?

Target represents the projected price objective associated with the setup. It should be treated as an analytical objective rather than a guarantee that the security will reach that price.

SCORE

What does the Score mean?

The score is a relative measure used by the system to rank trade setups according to its analysis framework. A higher score does not guarantee a successful trade.

STATUS

What do the setup statuses mean?

Setup status indicates where a trade currently stands within the system's publication and confirmation process. For example, setups may appear as pending or confirmed depending on the conditions detected by the system.

Using the Dashboard

DASHBOARD

What can I see on the Dashboard?

The Dashboard provides a high-level summary of the current published trade environment, including total trades, pending setups, confirmed setups, market bias, and the top trade setups.

TRADES

Where can I see all published trades?

Select TRADES from the navigation menu to view the complete published trade list rather than only the top setups displayed on the main dashboard.

UPDATES

How often does the information update?

Published information is updated as new system data becomes available. The dashboard's update indicator provides the latest publication or refresh information available to the website.

TOP 20

Why are only 20 setups shown on the Dashboard?

The Dashboard is designed as a summary view. The top 20 setups provide a concise view of the highest-ranked opportunities while the TRADES page provides access to the broader published trade set.

Risk & Interpretation

RISK

Does a confirmed setup guarantee a profitable trade?

No. A confirmed setup does not guarantee a profitable outcome. Market conditions can change rapidly, and every trade involves risk.

INVESTMENT

Is NEA28V1 financial advice?

No. NEA28V1 is provided for educational and informational purposes. The information presented by the system is not financial advice, investment advice, or a recommendation to buy or sell any security.

RISK MANAGEMENT

Why are stop-loss and target levels important?

Stop and target levels provide a framework for evaluating potential risk and reward before entering a trade. Traders should determine their own risk tolerance and position sizing before making any trading decision.

VOLATILITY

Can a setup change after it is published?

Yes. Market prices, volume, volatility, and technical conditions can change. A setup should therefore be evaluated using the latest available information rather than treated as a permanent signal.
